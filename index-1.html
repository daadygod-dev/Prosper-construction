About Us

Rwanda's Trusted
Construction Partner

Founded in Kigali, Prosper Construction Company Ltd has grown into one of Rwanda's most respected construction and engineering firms. For over a decade, we've transformed blueprints into lasting structures — from elegant homes to critical public infrastructure.

Our multidisciplinary team of engineers, architects, and skilled tradespeople combines local knowledge with international best practices to deliver projects that stand the test of time.

Our Mission

To deliver excellence in every project, building structures that empower communities and last generations.

Our Vision

To be the leading construction company in East Africa, setting benchmarks for quality, sustainability, and innovation.

Industry Expertise — Certified engineers with 14+ years of hands-on experience.
On-Time Delivery — Rigorous project management ensuring deadlines are met.
Transparent Pricing — No hidden costs. Detailed quotes before work begins.
